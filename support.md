# School Hive Support

School Hive connects a school with its educators and with learners' parents and guardians. Here's how to get help.

## Questions about a learner

Marks, attendance, timetables, fees and learners' details come from the school, and the school decides what's recorded. If something looks wrong, ask the school's office or your child's educator. The school can correct it.

## Signing in

- Your school sets up your account. Sign in with the phone number your school has on record, then enter the code we send you by SMS.
- No SMS? Wait a minute and try again. If it still doesn't arrive, ask your school's management for a one-time sign-in code, then tap **No SMS? Use a code from your school** on the sign-in screen.
- An account works on one phone at a time. Signing in on a new phone signs the old one out.
- New phone number? Ask your school to update it.

## Something wrong in a message, post or comment

- To report it, press and hold the message, or open the post's or comment's menu, and choose **Report**. Your school's management sees the report and can remove what was reported.
- To stop seeing someone's comments and direct messages, choose **Block** from one of their comments' menu, or press and hold one of their direct messages. You can undo this under **Settings → Blocked people**.

## The app isn't working

Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store, Google Play or AppGallery. If the problem continues, email us with your phone's make and model, what you were doing and what went wrong. Please don't include learners' personal information.

## Your account

- Change your name and photo under **Settings**.
- Delete your account under **Settings → Delete account**. The [privacy policy](https://moyazi-anthem.github.io/school-hive-legal/#deleting-your-account) explains what is deleted and what your school keeps.
- No longer have the app? Email us with the phone number of the account, and we'll delete it once we've confirmed it's yours.
