Privacy Policy for School Hive
Effective Date: August 2026

School Hive ("we," "us," or "our") provides this Privacy Policy to explain how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our school management and communication application. This policy is designed to comply with South African data protection standards (POPIA) and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect
To provide a secure and functional educational platform, we collect the following data:

Authentication Data: Names, email addresses, and phone numbers collected via Google Firebase Authentication to verify parents, educators, and administrators.

Educational Records: Learner names, student IDs, attendance logs, and informal assessment scores.

Communication Data: Messages sent through our in-app chat system and posts on the Global Feed, stored securely on our cloud database.

Device Information: Basic crash logs and usage analytics to improve app stability.

2. How We Use and Share Data
We use the collected data strictly to operate the School Hive platform and facilitate school communication.

Role-Based Access: Data is strictly siloed. Parents can only view their own child’s performance and attendance, while educators access data solely for their assigned teaching groups.

Service Providers: We utilize secure third-party services, primarily Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase, to host and process data securely. Personal data sent to Google may be shared with its affiliate companies.

No Data Selling: We do not and will never sell, rent, or trade personal or educational data to third parties. App data is never used for purposes unrelated to its educational function.

3. Security and Children's Privacy
Protecting user data, especially student information, is our highest priority.

Data Security: All data in transit and at rest is encrypted using industry-standard protocols to protect against unauthorized access.

Children's Data: While this app processes educational data, active accounts are operated by authorized adults (parents, educators, and administrators) rather than directly by young children.

Data Rights and Deletion: Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal and account data. Account deletion requests can be submitted by contacting their school administrator or reaching out directly to our support contact.
