# School Hive Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 12 November 2026**

School Hive is an app that connects a school with its staff and with learners' parents and guardians: marks, attendance, timetables, messages and school news. This policy explains what information School Hive holds, why, who can see it, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

**Our role.** Your school decides which school records it keeps about learners, parents and staff, and is responsible for them under POPIA. We process those records for the school, as its operator, and only to run School Hive for that school. For your School Hive account itself and for the public feed, we are responsible.

## Who uses School Hive

Accounts are for adults: parents and guardians, educators and school management. Learners don't sign in. Their information comes from their school.

## What we hold

**Your account**

- Your phone number, which you sign in with using a one-time code sent by SMS
- Your name and, if you add one, a profile photo
- An identifier for the phone you're signed in on (an account works on one phone at a time), and a code that lets us send that phone notifications
- When you last opened the app

**From your school**, including from SA-SAMS where the school links it to School Hive

- Learners' names, student and accession numbers, grades, classes and subjects
- Formal assessment tasks, marks and term results
- Parents' and staff members' phone numbers, which link each account to the right learners or classes
- Staff members' SA-SAMS login numbers, so that marks captured in School Hive are recorded in SA-SAMS under the educator who captured them
- Learners' ID photos, taken by the school

**Created as the school uses School Hive**

- Attendance registers, lesson notes, and notes staff make about how a learner did in a lesson (these notes are seen only by staff)
- Classwork marks, and marks captured in the app on their way into SA-SAMS
- Messages and attachments sent in chats, and the school's announcements
- Posts, comments and likes on the feed
- Gate card taps: the time a learner or staff member taps their card at the school gate
- For school management: whether School Hive has seen each educator that day (a gate tap, a marked register, or management marking them present). This is not an attendance record.
- Fee balances and payments, if the school records them in School Hive
- Reports you make about a message or post

**Error reports.** When something goes wrong in the app, it sends us a short error report: what failed, where in the app, your account ID if you're signed in, and the type of phone system. We don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

## How we use it

- To run School Hive for your school: dashboards, marks, attendance, timetables, messages and notifications
- To keep School Hive and the school's SA-SAMS in step: marks and tasks come from SA-SAMS, marks captured in School Hive go into SA-SAMS, and, where the school switches it on, learners' ID photos go both ways
- To send notifications, such as a learner arriving at the school gate or a new message
- To keep accounts secure and to fix problems

We never sell or rent personal information, never use it for advertising, and use school records only for the school's purposes.

## Who can see what

- **Parents and guardians** see only their own children: marks, classwork, attendance, gate arrivals, ID photo and fee balance.
- **Educators** see the learners at their school, and their own classes' work, lessons and registers.
- **School management** sees the whole school, including staff information.
- **The public feed.** Posts made by a school's management can be seen by anyone signed in to School Hive, including people at other schools. Only members of that school can comment, and the school's management can remove comments. Schools should only post photos of learners they have permission to share.
- **Our service providers**, who process information only for us:
  - Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): hosting, database, file storage, sign-in codes by SMS, and notifications
  - Huawei: notifications on Huawei phones
- **Your school's SA-SAMS**, on the school's own computer, where the school links it.
- Anyone the law requires us to share information with.

**Checking report cards.** When you scan a School Hive report card's code to check that it's genuine, the check happens on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

## Where it's stored

School Hive's database is in South Africa (Google Cloud, Johannesburg). Files such as photos and attachments, error reports, and some of the processing are on Google's servers in the United States. Huawei may deliver notifications to Huawei phones from outside South Africa. These providers are bound by data processing terms that protect personal information as POPIA requires for information sent outside South Africa (section 72).

## How long we keep it

- **Your account:** until you delete it.
- **School records:** for as long as your school uses School Hive, and as the school instructs. When a school stops using School Hive, we return or delete its records as agreed with the school.
- **Error reports:** only as long as we need them to fix problems.

## Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and object to how it's used.

- Change your name and photo in the app, under **Settings**.
- Delete your account in the app (see below).
- For school records, such as a learner's marks or details, ask your school, which decides what's kept.
- For anything else, email us at moyazitechnologies@gmail.com.

If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Regulator: [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za).

## Deleting your account

In the app, open **Settings → Delete account** and type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

If you no longer have the app, email moyazitechnologies@gmail.com with the phone number of the account. We'll delete it once we've confirmed the account is yours.

**Deleted:** your sign-in, profile and photo, your links to your children's records, your feed comments, likes and follows, and your place in your school's staff list.

**Kept by your school:** marks you captured (they're also in SA-SAMS), registers and lessons, messages you sent, posts made for the school, and the school's own list of phone numbers. Ask your school to remove your number from that list.

## Children's information

Learners don't have accounts. Their information comes from their school and is shown only to the school's staff and the learner's own parents or guardians. Learners' ID photos are taken by the school and shown only to staff and the learner's own parents or guardians.

## Keeping it safe

- Information travels encrypted, and Google stores it encrypted.
- Access rules on our servers decide what each account can see, so each person sees only what their role allows.
- An account works on one phone at a time. Signing in on another phone signs the old one out.
- Only the Moyazi Technologies staff who run School Hive can reach the systems behind it.
